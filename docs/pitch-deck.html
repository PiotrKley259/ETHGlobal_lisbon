01 · The problem

Holding ETH is easy. Protecting it isn't.

Options are opaque

Strikes, sigma, theta — priced in jargon, sized for professionals, alien to the people who actually need downside cover.

AI alone can't be trusted

An LLM that invents a price is a liability. Nobody should buy protection priced by vibes.

Settlement is a promise

Even a correct quote means little if payout depends on someone remembering to pay you.

OptoPuts: a chat-driven options desk where the agent computes nothing. deterministic, tested pricing code does the math off live Graph data, and Hedera turns the contract into a token that settles itself.

02 · Life of a trade

Worries in → audited transactions out

Ask

“Protect my ETH below $1,590 for the next 7 days.” Claude parses intent — and only intent.

Measure

Uniswap v3 swap history via The Graph becomes a realized-vol curve; Aave v3 sets the financing rate.

Price

Deterministic Black–Scholes with full Greeks, breakevens, payoff curve. Zero LLM arithmetic.

Mint

On confirm: coverage-gated HTS token per option series. The desk never sells what the treasury can't pay.

Settle

At expiry a worker pays max(0, K−S) in stablecoin — idempotent, logged to HCS, visible on Hashscan.

Frontend: React/Vite desk terminal · Backend: FastAPI + Claude tool-use agent · Chain: Node sidecar on Hedera testnet

03 · Bounty — Best AI Use Case of The Graph

The Graph is the load-bearing wall

Every number the desk shows is derived live from the gateway. No Graph, no product.

Uniswap v3 subgraph

Pool swap history → OHLC → realized volatility at 24h / 7d / 30d , annualized + per-window ± moves. Multi-asset: ETH, WBTC, AAVE, ... — including inverted-pool handling.

Aave v3 subgraph

USDC borrow market → continuously-compounded risk-free rate r feeding the pricer, with explicit fallback levels surfaced in the UI.

The agent reasons over it — it never computes

Claude classifies the vol regime (calm / elevated / stressed, percentile vs trailing 30d, user-tunable bands), chooses among 8 strategies to match the user's view, explains Greeks, and refuses uncovered mints. All arithmetic lives in tested Python tools.

05 · Bounty — Tokenization on Hedera

Financial contracts as protocol-native tokens

Each option series is an HTS token — no smart contract. The full derivative lifecycle runs on Hedera primitives:

Issue

TokenCreate per series (symbol encodes asset/type/strike), treasury-held, minted only after the coverage gate passes.

Hold & move

Atomic TransferTransaction to the buyer's real wallet; HIP-904 unlimited auto-association — the desk never touches the buyer's keys.

Settle & audit

Scheduled Transaction commitment at mint; expiry worker executes the stablecoin payout; every quote, trade, settlement logged to HCS.

Why it matters

This is the tokenization path for any cash-settled contract — options today; invoices, fund shares, carbon forwards tomorrow. Lifecycle + audit trail is exactly the compliance story HTS was built for.
